Based on the provided sources, the responsibilities of staff and students for waste reduction center on proper waste sorting at bins, participating in reuse initiatives, and engaging in laboratory waste reduction programs:
1. Correct Waste Sorting & Bin Usage
Staff and students are responsible for accurately segregating their waste across campus bin systems to prevent contamination of recycling and composting streams:
•	Smart Drink Container Bins (White): Empty, scan the barcode, and drop in eligible, uncrushed drink containers (plastic, glass, cans, juice boxes).
•	Food Organics Bins (Green): Place food leftovers, fruit/vegetable scraps, and coffee grounds into green office/kitchen caddies without any packaging or cutlery.
•	Glass, Metals, and Plastic Containers Bins (Yellow): Dispose of clean, empty glass bottles, steel/aluminum cans, aerosol cans, and hard plastic bottles.
•	Paper & Cardboard Bins (Blue): Ensure paper and cardboard placed here are clean and dry.
•	General Waste Bins (Red): Use for takeaway containers, soft plastics, coffee cups, cutlery, and tissues. If unsure where an item goes, staff and students are advised to use the red bin to avoid contaminating other recycling streams.
2. Prioritising Reuse and Waste Avoidance
•	Personal Reusables: Staff and students are encouraged to bring and use reusable coffee cups, food containers, and cutlery when dining on campus.
•	Furniture Reuse: Staff and departments participate in campus reuse systems, such as the furniture reuse program, before ordering new items.
3. Specialized Laboratory Waste Reduction
•	LEAF Program: Staff and student researchers in laboratories are encouraged to adopt the Laboratory Efficiency Assessment Framework (LEAF) to reduce single-use plastic consumption and energy/water waste in research spaces.
•	Lab Swap Events: Lab teams participate in University lab swap events to donate or claim unused, unexpired lab consumables (such as pipettes and tubes) and equipment, preventing usable supplies from being sent to waste streams.
4. Group & Faculty Engagement
•	Staff and students can participate in Faculty Sustainability Working Groups, Arc Go Green, or UNSW Urban Growers to promote waste reduction and sustainable habits across departments.
